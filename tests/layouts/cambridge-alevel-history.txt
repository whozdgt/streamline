Within each of the AS Level options there are three topics.
European option: Modern Europe, 1774–1924
1 France, 1774–1814
1.1 What were the causes and immediate consequences of the 1789 Revolution?
Causes, short-term and long-term, and the outbreak of revolution in 1789.
1.2 How and why did France become a republic by 1792?
The failure of constitutional monarchy and the flight to Varennes.
2 Liberalism and nationalism in Germany, 1815–71
2.1 What were the causes of the Revolutions of 1848?
Economic hardship and the hungry forties.
International option: International history, 1870–1939
3 International history 1929–39: the rise of extremism and the road to war
3.1 How did the rise of extremism affect international relations?
Fascist Italy and Nazi Germany, their aims in foreign policy.
3.2 Why did the League of Nations fail to keep the peace in the 1930s?
Manchuria and Abyssinia.
3.3 Why, and with what effects, did Britain and France pursue a policy of appeasement?
Rearmament and public opinion.
