2b. Content of A Level in Ancient History (H407)
Component Group 1 is a source-based Greek period study and a Greek depth study.
2c. Content of Greek period study (H407/11–13 entry code determined by Greek depth study selected)
Relations between Greek states and between Greek and non-Greek states, 492–404 BC
Learners will be required to demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the key historical terms and concepts.
Key topics Learners should have studied the following content:
The Persian
Wars
The causes of the Persian invasion of Greece; the battles of Marathon, Thermopylae and Salamis; the reasons for the Greek victory.
The Delian League
and Athenian empire
The foundation of the Delian League in 478 BC; its development into an Athenian empire; the revolts of Naxos and Thasos.
2c. Content of Greek depth studies
Learners study one of three depth studies.
Depth study in H407/11: The Society and Politics of Sparta, 478–404 BC
This depth study focuses on 5th century BC Sparta and develop an understanding
of the lives, contributions and experiences of the
different groups in Spartan society.
Key topics Learners should have studied the following content:
Education in
Sparta
The education of boys and men, including details of the organisation and stages of
the agoge; the education of girls; the values the agoge was intended to instil in
Spartans.
The social
structure of Sparta
The roles and status of Spartiates, perioikoi and helots; the relationship between the
helots and the Spartiates; the krypteia and the helot revolt of 464 BC.
Depth study in H407/12: The Culture and Politics of Athens, c. 460–c.399 BC
This depth study explores the concept of democracy and the consequential
freedom of speech and debate which were prized in Athens.
Key topics Learners should have studied the following content:
Athenian political
and social culture
The concepts of democracy and oligarchy; the nature and level of participation in the
democratic system by the population of Attica, including citizens, women and slaves.
Athenian religion
The festivals of Athens, including the Panathenaia and the City Dionysia; the role of
religion in the life of the city and the trial of Socrates.
Athenian art
The Parthenon frieze and its sculptures; the Erechtheion; red-figure pottery and what it shows about life.
Key topics Learners should have studied the following content:
Athenian drama
Tragedy and comedy at the City Dionysia, including Aristophanes and Euripides, and what plays show of the city.
