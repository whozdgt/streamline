2 Subject content
Paper 1: Area of Study 1 – Study of Religion
1A Catholic Christianity
Section 1: Beliefs and Teachings
1.1 The Trinity: the nature and significance of the Trinity as expressed in the Nicene Creed; the nature and significance of each of the Persons individually.
1.2 Creation: the account of creation in Genesis 1–3 and its interpretation by Catholics.
Section 2: Practices
2.1 The sacraments: the nature and importance of the sacramental nature of reality.
2.8 Catholic mission and evangelism: the history and significance of mission and evangelism for Catholics.
Section 3: Sources of Wisdom and Authority
3.1 The development and structure of the Bible as the revealed word of God, including the canon.
1C Islam
Section 1: Beliefs and Teachings
1.1 The six Beliefs of Islam: the nature, importance and purpose of the six beliefs in Sunni Islam.
1.2 The five roots of Usul ad-Din in Shi'a Islam: the nature and importance of the five roots.
Section 2: Practices
2.6 Hajj as one of the Five Pillars: the nature, role, origins and significance of Hajj for Muslims.
Section 3: Sources of Wisdom and Authority
3.1 The Qur'an: the revelation and compilation of the Qur'an and its significance and authority within Islam today.
