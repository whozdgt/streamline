3 Subject content
3.1 Themes
Students study all of the following themes on which the assessments are based.
3.1.1 Theme 1: People and lifestyle
Theme 1 covers the following three topics:
• Identity and relationships with others
• Healthy living and lifestyle
• Education and work
3.1.2 Theme 2: Popular culture
Theme 2 covers the following three topics:
• Free-time activities
• Customs, festivals and celebrations
• Celebrity culture
3.1.3 Theme 3: Communication and the world around us
Theme 3 covers the following three topics:
• Travel and tourism, including places of interest
• Media and technology
• The environment and where people live
3.2 Grammar
3.2.1 Foundation tier
3.2.1.1 Noun phrases
gender of nouns, singular and plural forms, definite and indefinite articles
3.2.1.2 Verb phrases
present tense of regular and irregular verbs, perfect tense with avoir and être, near future tense
3.2.2 Higher tier
3.2.2.1 Noun phrases
partitive articles, demonstrative pronouns
3.2.2.2 Verb phrases
pluperfect tense, conditional, subjunctive after il faut que
3.3 Vocabulary
3.3.1 Composition of vocabulary content
The vocabulary lists contain 1,200 items at Foundation tier and 1,700 at Higher tier.
1 Words are listed under the part of speech they belong to.
2 Because no vocabulary is specified for KS2 or KS3, the vocabulary list includes very common words.
3 The vocabulary lists include, as part of the 1,200 or 1,700 items, the words that make up a word family.
4 In addition to the 1,200 and 1,700 items, the vocabulary lists contain
the numbers and dates.
5 For Reading only
6 Words with multiple meanings but with the same part of speech (eg, the verb passer) count as one item.
