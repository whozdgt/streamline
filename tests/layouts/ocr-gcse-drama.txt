2 The specification overview
Learners must take all components to be awarded the OCR GCSE (9–1) in Drama.
2b. Content of GCSE (9–1) in Drama (J316)
The content of this GCSE in Drama is divided into three components.
Devising drama (01/02) Learners will research and explore a stimulus, work collaboratively and create their own devised drama.
Presenting and performing texts (03) Learners will develop and apply theatrical skills in acting or design by presenting a showcase of two extracts from a performance text.
Drama: Performance and response (04) Learners will explore practically a performance text to demonstrate their knowledge and understanding of drama.
2c. Content of Devising drama (01/02)
Devising drama
Learners should: Learners should know and understand
how to research and explore a stimulus: the different stimuli that may be used to create drama, and how to select and develop ideas
how to work collaboratively to develop ideas to communicate meaning: the ways in which drama is created and developed through rehearsal and improvisation
how to develop and use theatrical skills to communicate meaning in performance or design.
The performance must be at least 10 minutes long for a group of three learners.
2c. Content of Presenting and performing texts (03)
Learners should develop the skills to interpret a performance text for an audience, either as performers or designers.
Each extract, when performed, must have a performance time exceeding 1 minute.
Learners will need to develop and use their acting or design skills to realise their artistic intentions in a live performance.
2c. Content of Drama: Performance and response (04)
For this component, learners need to demonstrate knowledge and understanding of how drama is developed, performed and responded to.
Learners should: Learners must know and
understand
the characteristics of a performance text: genre, structure, character, form, style, language, stage directions
how meaning is communicated through the use of performance space and spatial relationships on stage
how to analyse and evaluate the work of others in a live theatre performance they have seen.
2d. Prior knowledge, learning and progression
Learners in England who are beginning a GCSE (9–1) course are likely to have followed a Key Stage 3 programme of study.
