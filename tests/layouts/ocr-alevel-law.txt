2b. Content of A Level in Law (H418)
The A Level in Law is made up of three mandatory components and a choice of one of two further components.
2c. Content of H418/01 – The legal system and criminal law
Section A: The legal system
Topic Learners should be able to demonstrate and apply their knowledge and understanding of:
Civil courts and
alternative dispute
resolution
The jurisdiction of civil courts, the appeal system and the use of tribunals, mediation, conciliation and arbitration.
Criminal courts and
lay people
Criminal process: bail, the jurisdiction of the magistrates and the Crown Court, the role of juries and magistrates.
Section B: Criminal law
Topic Learners should be able to demonstrate and apply their knowledge and understanding of:
General elements of
liability
Actus reus: voluntary conduct, omissions, causation; mens rea: intention, recklessness, transferred malice; strict liability.
Fatal offences
against the person
Murder and voluntary manslaughter: loss of control and diminished responsibility; involuntary manslaughter.
2c. Content of H418/02 – Law making and the law of tort
Section A: Law making
Topic Learners should be able to demonstrate and apply their knowledge and understanding of:
Parliamentary law
making
Green and White Papers, different types of Bill, the legislative process and the influences on Parliament.
Section B: The law of tort
Topic Learners should be able to demonstrate and apply their knowledge and understanding of:
Liability in
negligence
Duty of care: Donoghue v Stevenson (1932) and the neighbour principle, the Caparo test; breach of duty and damage.
2d. Legal Skills
Learners will be required to apply legal rules and principles to given scenarios.
