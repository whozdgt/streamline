5. Syllabus content
Candidates study the following topics.
5.1 Paper 1
1. Major themes of the Qur'an
Candidates should study the main themes of the Qur'an: God in himself, God's relationship with his created world, God's messengers.
They should know the passages listed in Appendix 1 and be able to explain their importance in Muslim thought.
2. The history and importance of the Qur'an
The revelation of the Qur'an to the Prophet between 610 and 632; its compilation under Abu Bakr and Uthman; its use as a source of law alongside the Hadith, consensus (ijma) and analogy
(qiyas)
3. The life and importance of the Prophet Muhammad (pbuh)
His life in Makka: his birth and early life, his marriage to Khadija, the first revelation, the persecution of the Muslims and the migration to Madina.
4. The first Islamic community
The Prophet's wives, his descendants, his companions, the first Muslims in Abyssinia and the first converts.
5.2 Paper 2
1. Major teachings in the Hadiths of the Prophet
Candidates should study the Hadiths in Appendix 2 and their teachings about the relationship between God and humankind, and between people.
2. The history and importance of the Hadiths
The compilation of the Hadiths, the main collections, the classification of a Hadith by its chain of transmitters (isnad) and its text (matn).
3. The period of rule of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and their importance as models of leadership
The lives and rule of Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali, their main achievements and the problems they faced.
4. The Articles of Faith and the Pillars of Islam
Belief in God, angels, books, prophets, the last day and predestination; the declaration of faith, prayer, almsgiving, fasting and pilgrimage.
