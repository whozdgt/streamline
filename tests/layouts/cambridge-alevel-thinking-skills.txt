1 Organise information

– rules that should be followed, such as the ways in which traffic moves through a junction.

2.1 Perform appropriate operations with information
• Use one or more items of information appropriately to solve a given problem
2.2 Identify cases that satisfy given criteria
• Search through all possible solutions to a problem
