2c. Development of practical skills in chemistry
Module 1: Development of practical skills in chemistry
1.1 Practical skills assessed in a written examination
1.1.1 Planning
(a) experimental design, including to solve problems set in a practical context
(b) identification of variables that must be controlled
1.1.2 Implementing
(a) how to use a wide range of practical apparatus and techniques
2d. Storylines
The content in this section is supported by the resources produced by the University of York.
Elements of life (EL)
The storyline considers the origin of the elements.
Atomic structure
(a) the terms atomic number, mass number, isotope
(b) the electronic structure of atoms
Modern analytical techniques
(c) use of data from a mass spectrum to determine relative abundance of isotopes
Developing fuels (DF)
The use of fuels in cars provides the main context in this storyline.
Energetics
(d) the terms: exothermic, endothermic, standard conditions
(e) the determination of enthalpy changes of reaction
Developing fuels (DF)
Bonding and structure
(f) the bonding in organic compounds in terms of sigma and pi bonds
Organic functional groups
(l) the terms aliphatic, aromatic, arene, saturated, unsaturated
2e. Chemical literacy (CL)
Learners are expected to demonstrate chemical literacy.
2f. Prior knowledge, learning and progression
Learners in England who are beginning an A Level course are likely to have followed a Key Stage 4 programme.
