# IGCSE Pakistan Studies notes written for this test: History (Paper 1) and Geography (Paper 2) revision notes. Each is
# under the point it is about; Geography's lettered points are named with their titles.
=== 1 | background to the movement
Cultural and historical background: the decline of the Mughal Empire, Shah Waliullah and Syed Ahmad Barelvi, the War of Independence of 1857, Sir Syed Ahmad Khan and the Aligarh Movement.
=== 2 | emergence of pakistan
The emergence of Pakistan: the Simla Deputation and the founding of the Muslim League in 1906, the Lucknow Pact, the Khilafat Movement, Allama Iqbal's Allahabad address, the Lahore Resolution of 1940, and partition in 1947.
=== 3 | nationhood
Nationhood 1947–99: the problems of partition, the death of Jinnah, Liaquat Ali Khan, the 1956 constitution, Ayub Khan's reforms, the separation of East Pakistan in 1971, Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto and Zia ul-Haq.
=== (d) Climate | climate
Climate of Pakistan: the monsoon from July to September, western depressions bringing winter rain to the north, temperature ranges in Jacobabad and Murree, rainfall decreasing from north to south.
=== (b) Forests | forests
Forests: coniferous forests in the northern mountains, riverine forests along the Indus, mangroves in the Indus delta; uses for timber and fuelwood; deforestation and afforestation.
=== (c) Renewables | renewable energy
Renewable power: hydroelectricity at Tarbela and Mangla dams, the wind corridor at Jhimpir and Gharo in Sindh, solar parks like Quaid-e-Azam Solar Park, their advantages for Pakistan.
=== (b) Crops and livestock | crops
Crops and livestock: wheat (rabi crop) and rice, cotton and sugarcane (kharif crops), the growing conditions for each; cattle, buffalo, sheep and goats, poultry.
=== (b) Secondary and tertiary industries | industry
Secondary and tertiary industries: the cotton textile industry, cement, steel at Port Qasim, cottage industries like carpet weaving; services such as banking and IT.
=== (a) Internal transport | roads and railways
Internal transport: the motorway network (M2 Lahore–Islamabad), the Karakoram Highway, Pakistan Railways, air travel within the country, CPEC road links.
=== (b) Movements of population | migration
Movements of population: rural to urban migration to Karachi and Lahore, push and pull factors, emigration to the Gulf states and the UK, remittances.
