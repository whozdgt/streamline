# GCSE English Literature notes written for this test: essay plans, quotes and annotations. Each is under the point it
# is about.
=== 3.1.1 | macbeth
Macbeth: ambition, the witches' prophecies, Lady Macbeth "unsex me here", the dagger soliloquy, guilt and the sleepwalking scene, kingship and the divine right of kings, Duncan vs Macbeth as kings.
=== 3.1.1 | romeo and juliet
Romeo and Juliet: love vs hate, fate ("star-crossed lovers"), the Prologue sonnet, Mercutio's Queen Mab speech, the balcony scene, Tybalt's death, the role of the Nurse and Friar Lawrence.
=== 3.1.2 | a christmas carol
A Christmas Carol: Scrooge's transformation, the three ghosts, Marley's chains, the Cratchits and Tiny Tim, Ignorance and Want, Dickens's criticism of the Poor Law and Malthus.
=== 3.1.2 | jekyll and hyde
Jekyll and Hyde: duality, Victorian respectability, Utterson as narrator, the Carew murder, Lanyon's narrative, Jekyll's full statement, Darwin and degeneration.
=== 3.2.1 | an inspector calls
An Inspector Calls: Priestley's socialist message, Mr Birling's dramatic irony about the Titanic, Sheila's change, Eva Smith, the generational divide, the Inspector's final speech "we are members of one body".
=== 3.2.2 | power and conflict
Power and conflict anthology: Ozymandias, London, My Last Duchess, Bayonet Charge, Exposure, Storm on the Island, Remains, Poppies, War Photographer, Kamikaze, comparing how poets present the effects of conflict.
=== 3.2.3 | unseen
Unseen poetry: read the poem twice, look at speaker, structure, imagery, form and rhyme, then compare the two unseen poems in part b.
