# GCSE Statistics notes written for this test: revision notes and worked examples. Each is under the point it is
# about, the lettered points named with their titles.
=== (a) Planning | planning an investigation
Planning a statistical investigation: writing a hypothesis, deciding what data to collect, identifying problems and constraints, the data handling cycle (plan, collect, process, interpret, evaluate).
=== (c) Population and sampling | sampling
Sampling methods: random sampling with a random number generator, systematic sampling, stratified sampling in proportion to group sizes, quota and cluster sampling, the sampling frame, bias in samples.
=== (d) Collecting data | questionnaires
Collecting data: designing a questionnaire with non-overlapping response boxes, avoiding leading questions, pilot surveys, interviews, controlled experiments and capture-recapture.
=== (a) Tabulation, diagrams and representation | diagrams
Representing data: pie charts, histograms with unequal class widths (frequency density), cumulative frequency graphs, stem and leaf diagrams, choosing an appropriate diagram, misleading graphs.
=== (b) Measures of central tendency | averages
Averages: mean, median and mode for raw data, and the estimated mean from grouped frequency tables using midpoints; weighted means; which average to use.
=== (c) Measures of dispersion | spread
Measures of spread: range, interquartile range, standard deviation, box plots and outliers (more than 1.5 x IQR beyond the quartiles), comparing two distributions.
=== (e) Scatter diagrams and correlation | correlation
Scatter diagrams: positive and negative correlation, a line of best fit through the mean point, Spearman's rank correlation coefficient, correlation does not mean causation.
=== (f) Time series | time series
Time series: plotting data over time, trend lines, seasonal variation, moving averages to smooth the data and predicting future values.
=== 3 | probability
Probability: sample spaces, Venn diagrams, tree diagrams for independent and dependent events, P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B) for independent events, expected frequency, risk.
