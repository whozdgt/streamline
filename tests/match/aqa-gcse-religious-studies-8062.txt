=== 3.1.2.1;3.1.3.1 | trinity and incarnation
Christian beliefs: the nature of God (omnipotent, loving, just), the Trinity of Father, Son and Holy Spirit, creation in Genesis 1, the incarnation, the crucifixion, resurrection and ascension, sin and salvation, the afterlife, heaven and hell.
=== 3.1.2.2 | christian practices
Christian practices: worship (liturgical, non-liturgical, informal), prayer and the Lord's Prayer, the sacraments of baptism and Holy Communion, pilgrimage to Lourdes and Iona, Christmas and Easter, the role of the church in the local community, food banks, mission and evangelism.
=== 3.1.5.1 | islam beliefs
Islamic beliefs: Tawhid (the oneness of God), the six articles of faith in Sunni Islam and the five roots of Usul ad-Din in Shi'a Islam, angels (Jibril), predestination, akhirah (life after death), risalah and the prophets (Adam, Ibrahim, Muhammad), the holy books including the Qur'an.
=== 3.1.5.2 | five pillars
The Five Pillars of Sunni Islam: Shahadah, Salah, Zakah, Sawm, Hajj; the Ten Obligatory Acts of Shi'a Islam; Jihad (greater and lesser); the festivals of Id-ul-Adha, Id-ul-Fitr and Ashura.
=== 3.1.1.1 | buddhist beliefs
Buddhism: the Dhamma, dependent arising, the three marks of existence (anicca, anatta, dukkha), the Buddha's life (the four sights, enlightenment), the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.
=== 3.1.6.2 | jewish practices
Judaism: the synagogue and its features (bimah, Aron Hakodesh, ner tamid), Shabbat at home and in the synagogue, the Amidah, brit milah, bar and bat mitzvah, marriage, mourning, kosher dietary laws, Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, Pesach.
=== 3.2.1.1 | relationships and families
Theme A: religious teachings about sex before marriage, contraception, same-sex relationships, marriage and cohabitation, divorce and remarriage, the nature and purpose of families, gender equality.
=== 3.2.1.2 | religion and life
Theme B: the origins of the universe (the Big Bang vs Genesis), the value of the world, stewardship and dominion, pollution, animal experimentation, the origins of human life and evolution, abortion, euthanasia, sanctity and quality of life.
=== 3.2.1.3 | existence of god
Theme C: the design argument (Paley's watch), the first cause argument, the argument from miracles, evil and suffering as an argument against God, special revelation (visions) and general revelation (nature, scripture).
=== 3.2.1.4 | peace and conflict
Theme D: peace, justice, forgiveness and reconciliation, violence and terrorism, reasons for war (greed, self-defence, retaliation), nuclear weapons, just war theory, holy war, pacifism, victims of war.
=== 3.2.1.5 | crime and punishment
Theme E: good and evil intentions, reasons for crime (poverty, addiction, greed), the aims of punishment (retribution, deterrence, reformation), the treatment of criminals, forgiveness, the death penalty.
=== 3.2.1.6 | human rights
Theme F: prejudice and discrimination, religious freedom, human rights, social justice, racial and gender prejudice, wealth and poverty, exploitation of the poor (people-trafficking, payday loans).
