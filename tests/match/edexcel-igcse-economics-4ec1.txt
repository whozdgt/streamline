=== 1.1.1 | scarcity
The economic problem: scarcity, finite resources and unlimited wants, opportunity cost, production possibility curves and shifts in the PPC.
=== 1.1.3 | demand and supply
Demand curves, movements along vs shifts, factors that shift supply, equilibrium price and quantity, excess demand and excess supply.
=== 1.1.4 | PED
Price elasticity of demand = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price, elastic and inelastic, the effect on total revenue, income elasticity, price elasticity of supply.
=== 1.1.6 | externalities
External costs and benefits of production and consumption, social cost = private cost + external cost, government responses: taxes, subsidies, regulation, tradable pollution permits.
=== 1.2.2 | specialisation
Productivity and division of labour: specialisation, advantages and disadvantages of the division of labour, labour productivity.
=== 1.2.4 | competition
Business competition: competitive markets, monopoly and oligopoly, barriers to entry, effects of competition on consumers and firms, mergers.
=== 1.2.5 | labour market
The labour market: demand for labour as a derived demand, supply of labour, wage determination, why wages differ, trade unions, the national minimum wage.
=== 2.1.1 | macro objectives
Macroeconomic objectives: economic growth (GDP), low unemployment, low and stable inflation (CPI), balance of payments stability, redistribution of income.
=== 2.1.2 | fiscal and monetary
Government policies: fiscal policy (taxation and government spending, budget deficits), monetary policy (interest rates set by the central bank), supply-side policies.
=== 2.2.2 | trade
International trade: advantages of free trade, protectionism (tariffs, quotas, subsidies), trade blocs, the World Trade Organisation.
=== 2.2.3 | exchange rates
Exchange rates: floating exchange rates, appreciation and depreciation, effects on imports, exports and inflation, SPICED and WPIDEC.
