# IGCSE Global Perspectives research notes written for this test. The syllabus is a list of topics to research rather
# than content, so each note is under the topic it researches.
=== Climate change, energy and resources | team project research
Our team project is about reducing energy use at school. Global perspective: rising CO2 emissions from fossil fuels, renewable energy targets. National perspective: our country's electricity comes mostly from coal. Local: the school uses lights and air conditioning in empty rooms. Possible action: switch-off campaign and posters.
=== Poverty and inequality | individual report notes
Research question: should governments do more to reduce poverty? Sources: World Bank extreme poverty line, Oxfam report on inequality, a local food bank. Perspectives: some say aid creates dependency, others that it saves lives. Evaluate sources for bias and expertise.
=== Migration and urbanisation | written exam practice
Source A says migrants to cities find work but live in informal settlements. Causes of rural to urban migration: push and pull factors. Consequences: overcrowding, pressure on services, but also economic growth.
=== Digital world | issue notes
The digital divide between rich and poor countries, social media and mental health, misinformation online, the right to privacy, internet access as a human right.
=== Health and wellbeing | research notes
Obesity rates are rising globally; sugar taxes in Mexico and the UK; access to healthcare; mental health stigma among young people; vaccination programmes.
=== Water, food and agriculture | group notes
Water scarcity, food security, food waste in supermarkets, intensive farming vs organic farming, GM crops, desertification.
=== Conflict and peace | perspectives
Causes of conflict: resources, religion, ethnicity. Role of the United Nations peacekeeping. Refugees fleeing conflict. Can conflict ever be justified?
