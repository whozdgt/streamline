# International GCSE Mathematics A notes written for this test: worked examples and revision notes. Each is under the
# topic it is about.
=== 1 | fractions and percentages
Fractions: equivalent fractions, adding fractions with different denominators, multiplying and dividing mixed numbers. Percentages: percentage increase and decrease, reverse percentages, compound interest 1.05^n, standard form 3.2 x 10^5.
=== 1 | surds and bounds
Recurring decimals to fractions (x = 0.272727..., 100x - x), surds: simplify root 50 = 5 root 2, rationalising the denominator, upper and lower bounds of a calculation.
=== 2 | solving equations
Linear equations with brackets and fractions, simultaneous equations by elimination and substitution, quadratic equations by factorising, the quadratic formula, completing the square, linear inequalities on a number line.
=== 2 | rearranging formulae
Changing the subject of a formula, expanding brackets, factorising expressions, algebraic fractions, index laws with negative and fractional powers.
=== 3 | graphs
Straight line graphs y = mx + c, gradient and intercept, sequences and the nth term of an arithmetic sequence, function notation f(x), inverse and composite functions, differentiation to find turning points.
=== 4 | geometry
Angles in polygons, circle theorems (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference, alternate segment theorem), Pythagoras' theorem, SOHCAHTOA, the sine rule and cosine rule, area of a sector, volume of a cone and sphere.
=== 5 | vectors
Column vectors, adding and subtracting vectors, vector proofs with parallel lines, transformations: reflection, rotation, enlargement with fractional scale factor, translation by a vector.
=== 6 | statistics and probability
Mean, median and mode from a frequency table, cumulative frequency and box plots, histograms with frequency density, tree diagrams, conditional probability, Venn diagrams.
=== - | history
The Treaty of Versailles: war guilt clause, reparations of £6.6 billion.
=== - | biology
Enzymes are denatured above their optimum temperature.
