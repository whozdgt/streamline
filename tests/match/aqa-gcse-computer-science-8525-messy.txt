=== 3.1.3,3.1.4 | search sort
linear search: check each item in turn, works on unsorted. binary search: list must be sorted, look at middle, discard half, repeat. much faster on big lists. bubble sort: compare pairs, swap, passes til no swaps. merge sort: split into single items then merge in order, faster but more memory
