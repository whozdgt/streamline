=== 1.1;1.2 | types of tourism
Types of tourism: leisure, business, visiting friends and relatives (VFR), domestic, inbound and outbound tourism; reasons people travel such as relaxation, sport, culture, health tourism and adventure.
=== 1.3;2.5 | sustainable tourism
Sustainable tourism: ecotourism in Costa Rica, carrying capacity, community-based tourism, reducing the carbon footprint of flights, protecting fragile environments.
=== 2.6 | impacts
Impacts of tourism: economic (jobs, multiplier effect, leakage), environmental (erosion, pollution, habitat loss) and sociocultural (commodification of culture, the demonstration effect) impacts on Bali.
=== 3.3 | transport
Types of transport: low-cost airlines vs full-service carriers, high-speed rail like the Eurostar, cruise ships and ferries, coach travel, their appeal to different tourists.
=== 4.1;4.2 | customer service
Customer service: why it matters (repeat business, reputation), meeting customer needs, dealing with complaints, the skills of a hotel receptionist, communication with customers with special needs.
=== 5.3 | marketing mix
The marketing mix for a theme park: product, price (seasonal pricing, discounts), place (online booking, travel agents), promotion (social media, brochures, TV adverts).
=== 5.4;5.5 | market research
Market research: questionnaires, focus groups, secondary research, SWOT and PEST analysis, market segmentation by age, income and lifestyle, targeting families vs young couples.
