# IGCSE Enterprise notes written for this test: revision notes and parts of an enterprise project. Each is under the
# point it is about.
=== 1.1 | what is enterprise
What is meant by enterprise: having an idea and taking the initiative and the risk to make it happen; entrepreneurs, enterprising activity in business and in the community.
=== 2.2 | types of business
Types of business organisation: sole trader, partnership, private and public limited companies, social enterprises and charities; unlimited and limited liability.
=== 3.1 | skills of enterprising people
Skills of enterprising people: problem solving, team working, communication, leadership, planning, time management, creativity and resilience.
=== 4.2 | risk
Risk in an enterprise project: what could go wrong (no customers, losing money, stock going out of date), how likely each risk is, and how to reduce it, e.g. a risk assessment.
=== 5.1 | negotiation
The negotiation process: preparing, knowing what you want and your limits, discussing, bargaining, reaching agreement and confirming it, e.g. negotiating a price with a supplier.
=== 6.1 | sources of finance
Sources of finance for an enterprise: personal savings, family and friends, bank loans and overdrafts, grants, crowdfunding, and their advantages and disadvantages.
=== 6.4 | financial records
Financial records: a cash flow forecast, an income statement (revenue minus costs gives profit), keeping receipts and invoices, break-even.
=== 7.3 | business plan
A business plan: the business idea, aims and objectives, market research findings, the marketing plan, financial forecasts, why banks ask to see one.
=== 8.2 | market research
Market research: primary research (questionnaires, interviews, observation, test marketing) and secondary research (internet, government statistics), sampling.
=== 8.4 | marketing communications
Marketing communications: advertising, social media, leaflets and posters, sales promotions, choosing the right method for the target market.
=== 10.2 | meetings
Meetings and presentations: agendas, minutes, chairing a meeting, preparing a presentation with slides to pitch our idea, speaking clearly to an audience.
