# GCSE Media Studies notes written for this test: CSP analyses, theory summaries and exam answers. Each is under the
# point it is about.
=== 3.4.3 | narrative
Narrative theory: Todorov's equilibrium, disruption, recognition, repair and new equilibrium; Propp's character types (hero, villain, donor, princess); Levi-Strauss's binary oppositions; applied to a trailer for a crime drama.
=== 3.4.5 | genre conventions
Codes and conventions of a music video and a magazine front cover: masthead, cover lines, the main image, mode of address, genre conventions, hybridity, Neale's idea that genres change over time.
=== 3.5.2 | gender
Theoretical perspectives on gender: Mulvey's male gaze, Gauntlett's idea of identity and pick and mix, Van Zoonen on the representation of women, how men are represented in adverts for aftershave.
=== 3.5.5 | stereotypes
Stereotypes: how teenagers are stereotyped in the news, positive and negative stereotypes, countertypes, Hall's representation theory, selection and construction of reality.
=== 3.6.3 | ownership
Media ownership: conglomerates like Disney and News Corp, vertical and horizontal integration, public service broadcasting (the BBC) vs commercial broadcasting, the impact of ownership on content.
=== 3.6.7 | regulation
Regulation: Ofcom, IPSO, the BBFC and age ratings, PEGI for video games, the Leveson Inquiry, the challenges of regulating online media.
=== 3.6.8 | digital tech
Digital technologies: convergence, streaming services like Netflix, social media distribution, the impact of the internet on newspapers, user-generated content.
=== 3.7.1 | uses and gratifications
Audience theories: Blumler and Katz's uses and gratifications (information, entertainment, personal identity, social interaction), Bandura's media effects, Gerbner's cultivation theory, Hall's reception theory (dominant, negotiated, oppositional readings).
=== 3.7.3 | targeting
Targeting audiences: demographics and psychographics, mainstream and niche audiences, how a video game like Fortnite targets its audience, marketing and promotion.
=== 3.8.1.1 | NEA statement of intent
NEA: writing the statement of intent (300 words) for a magazine front cover and double page spread, explaining how the production meets the brief and targets the audience.
