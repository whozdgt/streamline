# GCSE Drama notes written for this test: revision notes, a rehearsal log and a theatre review. Each is under the point
# it is about.
=== 3.1.1 | stage types
Stage configurations: proscenium arch, thrust, traverse, theatre in the round, promenade and end-on. Stage positions: upstage, downstage, stage left and right. Roles in the theatre: playwright, director, performer, designers, technicians, stage manager.
=== 3.1.2 | set play
Blood Brothers set play notes: how I would play Mrs Johnstone in the scene where she gives Edward away, use of voice, facial expression and proxemics; the narrator's role; the social context of 1980s Liverpool.
=== 3.1.3 | live theatre review
Live theatre review: we saw The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time. The actor playing Christopher used physical theatre and the grid-like set; analyse and evaluate how the performers communicated his anxiety to the audience.
=== 3.2.1 | devising
Devising log: our stimulus was a photograph of an empty playground. We researched, improvised, used Frantic Assembly chair duets and Brecht's breaking the fourth wall, then rehearsed and refined the piece.
=== 3.3 | texts in practice
Texts in practice: performing two extracts from a play to a visiting examiner, learning lines, interpreting the character and making sure the extracts show different skills.
=== 3.4.2 | lighting
Lighting design: fresnel and profile spots, gels for colour, gobos to project patterns, a cyclorama wash, cross-fades and blackouts on the cue sheet to show time of day.
=== 3.4.3 | sound
Sound design: recorded sound effects and live sound, underscoring music to build tension, volume and speaker placement, a sound cue sheet and when each effect is triggered.
=== 3.4.4 | set design
Set design: a model box, flats and rostra for levels, a composite set for several locations, minimalist versus naturalistic sets, furniture and set dressing.
=== 3.4.5 | costume
Costume design: choosing fabric, colour and period details for each character, distressing a costume to show poverty, hair and make-up, quick changes.
