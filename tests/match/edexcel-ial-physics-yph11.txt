# International Advanced Level Physics notes written for this test: revision notes, practical write-ups and exam
# answers. Each is under the point it is about.
=== 1.3 | suvat and moments
Equations of motion for uniform acceleration (suvat), projectiles, Newton's laws, moments and the principle of moments, centre of gravity, kinetic and gravitational potential energy, work done, power and efficiency.
=== 1.4 | materials
Materials: density, Stokes' law and viscosity, upthrust, Hooke's law, stress, strain and the Young modulus, stress-strain graphs, elastic and plastic deformation, elastic strain energy.
=== 2.3 | waves
Waves: transverse and longitudinal, the wave equation v = f lambda, superposition and standing waves, diffraction and interference, refraction and total internal reflection, the photoelectric effect, photons and electron diffraction.
=== 2.4 | circuits
Electric circuits: current as the rate of flow of charge, potential difference, resistance and resistivity, I-V characteristics, series and parallel, potential dividers, internal resistance and emf.
=== 4.4 | fields
Electric fields and capacitors: Coulomb's law, field strength E = F/Q, capacitance, charging and discharging (time constant RC). Magnetic fields: flux density, F = BIl, electromagnetic induction, Faraday's and Lenz's laws.
=== 4.5 | particles
Nuclear and particle physics: alpha particle scattering, particle accelerators (linac and cyclotron), quarks and leptons, conservation laws in particle interactions, matter and antimatter.
=== 5.3 | thermal
Thermodynamics: specific heat capacity, specific latent heat, internal energy, absolute zero, the ideal gas equation pV = NkT, kinetic theory.
=== 5.4 | radioactivity
Nuclear decay: alpha, beta and gamma radiation, background radiation, half-life, decay constant, the exponential decay equation.
=== 5.5 | SHM
Oscillations: simple harmonic motion, a = -omega^2 x, mass-spring systems and simple pendulums, damping, forced oscillations and resonance.
=== 5.6 | astro
Astrophysics and cosmology: gravitational fields, Newton's law of gravitation, the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, Stefan-Boltzmann law, Wien's law, standard candles, red shift, Hubble's law and the age of the universe.
=== - | chemistry
Le Chatelier: increasing temperature favours the endothermic direction.

Ansoff's matrix - market penetration vs diversification.
