# GCSE Astronomy notes written for this test: revision notes and observation write-ups. Each is under the points it is
# about.
=== 1.1;1.3;1.6 | the earth
The Earth: an oblate spheroid, its internal structure (crust, mantle, outer core, inner core), the atmosphere and light pollution affecting observations.
=== 2.4;2.5;2.6;2.8 | the moon
The lunar disc: maria (Sea of Tranquility, Sea of Crises), craters like Tycho and Copernicus, the lunar highlands, synchronous rotation so we always see the same side, libration lets us see 59% of the surface.
=== 3.8;3.9;3.10 | eclipses
Eclipses: solar eclipses (total, partial, annular), the umbra and penumbra, the diamond ring effect, lunar eclipses and why the Moon turns red, why eclipses don't happen every month.
=== 4.1;4.9;4.10 | lunar phases
Sidereal and synodic months, the lunar phase cycle from new moon to waxing crescent, first quarter, gibbous, full moon and back.
=== 4.2;4.3;4.5;4.8 | time and sundials
Apparent solar time and mean solar time, the equation of time and why it varies through the year, using a sundial and the gnomon, finding local noon from the Sun's shadow.
=== 3.3 | eratosthenes
Eratosthenes measured the circumference of the Earth from the angle of shadows at Syene and Alexandria; Aristarchus estimated the relative distances of the Sun and Moon.
