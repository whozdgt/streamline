=== 3.1.1 | plate margins
destructive: oceanic goes under continental (subduction), volcanoes + earthquakes eg andes. constructive: plates move apart, magma rises, mid atlantic ridge, iceland. conservative: slide past, earthquakes only, san andreas
=== 3.1.1 | nepal 2015 vs chile 2010 case study
Nepal 7.8 magnitude, 9000 died, poor building, landslides blocked roads, aid slow. Chile 8.8 but only ~500 died, building regs, quick response. LIC vs HIC effects & responses. primary vs secondary effects table
=== 3.1.1 | typhoon haiyan
tropical storm philippines 2013. forms over warm ocean >27C, coriolis effect makes it spin, eye in middle. storm surge 5m, 6000 dead. climate change might make them stronger + more frequent
=== 3.1.1 | uk weather
UK extreme weather: somerset levels flooding 2014, beast from the east snow 2018. is the weather getting more extreme? evidence both ways
=== 3.1.1 | climate change ev
evidence: ice cores, tree rings, temperature records since 1850. natural causes = orbital changes (milankovitch), volcanic eruptions, solar output. human = burning fossil fuels, farming, deforestation. mitigation vs adaptation
=== 3.1.2 | food web pond
small scale ecosystem: pond. producers (reeds, algae) -> consumers (snails, frogs, heron). decomposers recycle nutrients. if you remove one species the whole web changes
=== 3.1.2 | amazon notes
tropical rainforest: hot wet all year, layers (emergent canopy undercanopy shrub), buttress roots, drip tips. deforestation for cattle ranching, logging, mining, roads. sustainable management: selective logging, ecotourism, debt reduction
=== 3.1.2 | sahara or tundra?? q
hot desert: camels, cacti store water, long roots. opportunities in thar desert: mining, farming w irrigation, tourism. challenges: extreme temps, water supply, inaccessibility. desertification causes overgrazing, climate change
=== 3.1.3 | coasts
erosion processes hydraulic action, abrasion, attrition, solution. landforms: headland + bay, cave arch stack stump (old harry). longshore drift moves sediment, spit forms eg spurn head. hard engineering sea walls groynes vs soft beach nourishment
=== 3.1.3 | rivers exam answer
upper course: v shaped valley, interlocking spurs, waterfall (high force) - hard rock over soft, plunge pool, undercut, collapse, gorge. lower course: meanders, ox-bow lakes, floodplain, levees. hydrograph: lag time, peak discharge
=== 3.2.1 | lagos / rio

=== 3.2.1 | london regen

=== 3.2.2 | development
measuring development: GNI per head, HDI, birth rate, life expectancy. demographic transition model 5 stages. uneven development causes: physical (landlocked), economic (trade), historical (colonialism). closing the gap: aid, fair trade, microfinance, tourism (jamaica)
=== 3.2.2 | nigeria NEE

=== 3.2.2 | uk economy

=== 3.2.3 | food water energy

=== 3.2.3 | water q
