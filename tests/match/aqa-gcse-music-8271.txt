# GCSE Music notes written for this test: listening notes on the study pieces and areas of study, performance and
# composition notes. Each is under the point it is about.
=== 3.1.2 | haydn
Haydn's Clock Symphony, second movement: the classical orchestra, the ticking accompaniment in bassoons and strings, sonata and rondo form, the change to the minor key, balanced four-bar phrases.
=== 3.1.4 | the beatles
The Beatles, Sgt Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band: With a Little Help from My Friends, Within You Without You (sitar and tabla), Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds; studio effects, the rock band line-up, verse and chorus structure.
=== 3.1.5 | paul simon
Paul Simon's Graceland: Graceland, Diamonds on the Soles of Her Shoes and You Can Call Me Al; South African township music, the fretless bass, isicathamiya vocal style with Ladysmith Black Mambazo, fusion of styles.
=== 3.1.6 | copland
Copland's Rodeo: Saturday Night Waltz and Hoe-Down; American folk tunes like Bonyparte, the orchestration with xylophone and woodblock, syncopation; music since 1910 like minimalism and impressionism.
=== 3.1.3,3.1.7 | musical language
Musical language: cadences (perfect, imperfect, plagal, interrupted), major and minor keys, tempo words like allegro and andante, dynamics, ostinato, sequence, ternary form, reading treble and bass clef.
=== 3.2.1 | performing
Performance: one solo and one ensemble piece, four minutes in total. My grade 4 piano piece — accuracy of notes and rhythm, tone, dynamics and phrasing, keeping in time with the other players in the ensemble.
=== 3.3.1 | composition
Composition: two compositions, one to a brief set by the board. Documenting it with a score or a lead sheet and annotations explaining melody, harmony, structure and instrumentation.
