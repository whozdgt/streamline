Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026. Applies to Streamline on Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Linux and Windows.

Streamline is a music player for music you already have (files on your device, or a server you run yourself), with study tools: Pods, which sort your notes and match them to your exam board's specification, flashcards and to-dos. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is sold or shared for advertising.

Without an account

You can use Streamline without an account. Your music, playlists, listening history, documents, pods, flashcards, to-dos and settings then stay on your device. Your documents are never uploaded: Pods read and sort them on your device, and the AI that does it runs on your device too. These requests carry no account or device identifier. Like any internet request, they show your IP address to the service that answers it.

With an account

An account is optional. It lets you keep playlists across devices and follow friends. It is made with a username and a password only: no email address, phone number or real name is asked for. Accounts are stored by Supabase, which runs Streamline's database and file storage.

An account stores:

  • your username and your password (kept only as a secure hash);
  • a display name, short bio, profile picture and banner, if you add them;
  • playlists you save to your account (their names and each song's title, artist, album and length);
  • your to-dos and flashcards, so they sync between your devices (and with Dayline);
  • who you follow, follow requests, and people you block;
  • reports you make about other accounts, so they can be reviewed;
  • the song you're playing right now, but only while you've switched on sharing what you listen to;
  • your privacy choices (for example whether your profile is public).

What other people can see depends on your choices: a private profile shows only your name and picture, and only people you accept can see more. Your music files are never uploaded.

Why it's used

Only to make the features above work. It is not used for advertising, profiling or tracking, and it is not sold.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Account data is kept until you delete the account. Deleting it removes your profile, pictures, playlists, follows, blocks, reports and listening status straight away; nothing is kept afterwards.

  • In the app: open your profile and choose Delete account.
  • Without the app: use the delete your account page.
  • You can also download a copy of your account data in the app (profile, Export my data).

Security

Connections to Streamline's database, lyrics and artist pictures use encryption (https). A media server you add yourself is reached the way you set it up, which at home is often plain http on your own network.

Children

Streamline isn't made for children under 13, and accounts aren't meant for them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub, or use the contact address on the app's store page.
